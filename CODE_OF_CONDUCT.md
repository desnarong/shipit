# Code of Conduct

We want shipit to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Do:
- Be respectful, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith and ask when something is unclear.

Don't:
- Harass, insult, demean or discriminate against anyone.
- Publish others' private information.
- Spam, troll, or derail discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, issues, commits and pull requests that violate this code,
and may ban contributors who do so repeatedly. To report a problem, open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/desnarong/shipit/security/advisories/new) marked "conduct"
or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1.
